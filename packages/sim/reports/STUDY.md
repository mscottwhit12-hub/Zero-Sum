# Player-type study

Settings `recommended`. 6000 simulated nights (3000 at 12 players, 3000 at 20). Each night is a random mix of the 27 player types; each player is randomly low, mid or high skill. A pump crew and a kingmaker crew (3 players each) each appear in about half of nights.

**Edge** = average final gold minus the average of *Never trades* players with the same skill in the same party size. ★ = clear effect (more than 2 standard errors from zero); no star means it could be noise.

## Findings

- **Low-skill players:** best strategies are Fee-aware minimalist (+11.1 gold), Crown chaser (+8.7 gold), Active trader (+6.8 gold); worst are Self-pumper (-21.2), Contrarian (-9.4), Momentum chaser (-7.4).
- **Mid-skill players:** best strategies are Fee-aware minimalist (+11.8 gold), Crown chaser (+9.4 gold), Active trader (+8.5 gold); worst are Self-pumper (-21.4), Contrarian (-9.1), Event-stock speculator (-7.0).
- **High-skill players:** best strategies are Fee-aware minimalist (+12.3 gold), Crown chaser (+8.3 gold), Active trader (+7.9 gold); worst are Self-pumper (-22.5), Contrarian (-10.7), Momentum chaser (-8.3).
- ⚠️ **Possible exploit:** Active trader at low skill ends 16% ahead of never-trades (+6.8 gold).
- ⚠️ **Possible exploit:** Active trader at mid skill ends 18% ahead of never-trades (+8.5 gold).
- ⚠️ **Possible exploit:** Crown chaser at low skill ends 21% ahead of never-trades (+8.7 gold).
- ⚠️ **Possible exploit:** Crown chaser at mid skill ends 20% ahead of never-trades (+9.4 gold).
- ⚠️ **Possible exploit:** Crown chaser at high skill ends 15% ahead of never-trades (+8.3 gold).
- ⚠️ **Possible exploit:** Fee-aware minimalist at low skill ends 26% ahead of never-trades (+11.1 gold).
- ⚠️ **Possible exploit:** Fee-aware minimalist at mid skill ends 25% ahead of never-trades (+11.8 gold).
- ⚠️ **Possible exploit:** Fee-aware minimalist at high skill ends 23% ahead of never-trades (+12.3 gold).
- **Low-skill shot at the podium:** never-trades reaches top 3 in 4% of nights; the best strategies are Fee-aware minimalist 31%, Active trader 20%, Crypto gambler 20%.
- **Skill vs strategy:** a high-skill never-trader wins 10% of nights, a low-skill never-trader 0%. The best low-skill strategy wins 11%.
- **Games vs phone time (mid skill):** Game grinder plays 4.5 games (+2.3 gold vs never-trades); Social butterfly plays 3.4 (+2.0, trading +6.4); never-trades plays 4.1.
